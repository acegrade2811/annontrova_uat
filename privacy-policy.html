1. Introduction and Scope

Annotrova (“Annotrova”, “we”, “us”) provides AI data operations and data annotation services, including image, video, text and NLP, audio and 3D data annotation and data quality assurance. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website, annotrova.in.

It applies to visitors to the website and to people who contact us through it. Information handled as part of a client project is governed by the written agreement for that project, in addition to this policy.

2. Information Collected Through the Website

We keep data collection limited. We collect:

  • Information you choose to submit through the contact form (see the next section).
  • Information you share when you email or call us, such as your name, contact details and the contents of your message.
  • Basic technical information that web servers and browsers commonly exchange, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, which may be recorded in hosting server logs.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the website is not directed at them.

3. Personal Information Submitted Through the Contact Form

When you submit the inquiry form, we receive:

  • Full name
  • Company name
  • Business email address
  • Service required
  • Project details you choose to write

Please do not include confidential datasets, sensitive personal data or login credentials in the form. We will agree a secure way to share project data after we have discussed your requirements.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we receive to:

  • Respond to inquiries and prepare quotations or proposals.
  • Understand your project and recommend suitable services.
  • Manage our business relationship and communications with you.
  • Operate, secure and improve the website.
  • Meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Communication and Business Inquiries

We will use your contact details to reply to your inquiry and to follow up on the discussion. We will contact you for business purposes related to your request. If you would like us to stop contacting you, write to info@annotrova.in and we will honour your request, except for communications we must send to manage an existing contract or meet legal duties.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

At the time of writing, the website does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not use third-party analytics. Hosting providers may record basic server logs, and your browser may store technical data needed to display the site.

If we later add analytics or non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

8. Data Storage, Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We keep inquiry information only for as long as needed to respond, manage the business relationship, and meet legal or accounting requirements. Unless a longer period is required, we aim to delete or anonymize inquiries that do not lead to an engagement within [retention period, e.g. 24 months].

9. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We share personal information only when needed:

  • With service providers who help us operate the website and handle inquiries, as described above.
  • With professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, under confidentiality duties.
  • When required by law, court order or a lawful request from a government authority.
  • In connection with a business restructuring or sale, with notice where required.

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

10. Your Rights and Requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your information, where we have no legal reason to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, where processing is based on consent.

Send requests to info@annotrova.in. We may need to verify your identity before acting, and we will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law.

11. Data Protection and Indian Laws

We handle personal information in line with Indian laws that apply to our business, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, to the extent applicable. Our commitment is to process personal data lawfully, for the purposes described here, and with reasonable safeguards.

Where we process personal data on behalf of a client during a project, the client is generally responsible for having the right to share that data, and our duties are set out in our written agreement with them.

12. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the website after an update means you accept the revised policy.
